Status meanings:

- `apply`: generally safe when the checklist is in scope;
- `confirm`: enforce only after checking the current template or higher-tier source;
- `review`: semantic or visual judgment is required;
- `reframed`: retain the legitimate objective but reject the stated rationale or implementation.

Methods are `D` deterministic, `S` semantic, `V` visual, and `M` manual.
